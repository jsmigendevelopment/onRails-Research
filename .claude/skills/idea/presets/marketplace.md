# Preset: Marketplace

**For:** two sides of users, with payments between them.

## Preloaded (confirm, don't ask)

- **Add-ons:** Clerk auth, Stripe Connect (payouts to sellers), R2 uploads (listings and photos), Resend email, Sentry, Cloudflare Web Analytics, Turnstile.
- **Floor answers:**
  - both sides sign up;
  - reporting and blocking users;
  - refunds and disputes go through Stripe;
  - people can delete their account and their data;
  - WCAG 2.2 AA.
- **Common slices:** sign-up for each side, create a listing, browse and search, checkout, payouts, messaging or reviews.
- **Risk:** payments, payouts, uploads and user-to-user content are the risky lane.

## Still ask

- The fee model: commission, listing fee or subscription. Ask this open first.
- Which side you need first, and how you'll get them (the cold-start plan shapes the MVP).
- Whether the two sides talk directly, and how you'll moderate it.
